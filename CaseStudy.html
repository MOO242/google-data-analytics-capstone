Introduction (ASK Phase) Bellabeat is a high‑tech wellness company that manufactures smart devices focused on women’s health. The goal of this case study is to analyze smart‑device usage data to identify trends in user activity, sleep, and daily habits. These insights will help Bellabeat strengthen its marketing strategy and support the growth of the Leaf wellness tracker.

SHARE Phase: Communicating Insights

The analysis reveals several important trends in user behavior:

  • Users are generally not meeting recommended activity levels, averaging only 6,547 steps per day.
  • Sedentary behavior is high, with a median of 1,057 sedentary minutes per day.
  • Sleep duration is slightly below recommended levels, and sleep efficiency varies significantly.
  • Activity and sleep show only weak correlations, suggesting that users may need more structured guidance.
  • Weekday patterns indicate opportunities for targeted engagement.

These insights help Bellabeat understand how users interact with their wellness data and where behavior improvements can be encouraged.

ACT Phase: Recommendations for Bellabeat

Based on the analysis, several strategic actions can help Bellabeat improve user engagement and support healthier habits:

1. Promote increased physical activity

Bellabeat can introduce step‑based challenges, personalized reminders, and motivational notifications to help users reach the recommended 10,000 steps per day.

2. Support better sleep habits

Since sleep efficiency varies widely, Bellabeat can offer bedtime routine guidance, sleep hygiene tips, and insights when users spend too much time awake in bed.

3. Personalize engagement based on weekday patterns

Activity and sleep behaviors differ across the week. Bellabeat can tailor notifications and coaching to match these patterns.

4. Highlight holistic wellness features

Marketing should emphasize stress tracking, mindfulness, hydration reminders, and cycle tracking — features that align with user needs.

5. Build data‑driven marketing campaigns

Insights from real user behavior can shape campaigns that resonate with women seeking healthier routines.

These actions can help Bellabeat strengthen its brand, improve user engagement, and support long‑term wellness outcomes.
